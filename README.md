## About

I'm **Ramez Al-Maqtari**, a Cybersecurity student from Yemen. I come from a design and product background (web platforms, SaaS, client websites), and I'm now training hands-on in defensive security.

This repo documents that journey from day one. Every lab, course and investigation I complete gets a short write-up here, in my own words: what I did, what I found, and what I learned.

> **Why public?** Writing things up forces me to understand them. It also shows how I think, not just which certificates I hold.
